👋 About Me

My name is Hao Zhang. I am a master's student at the Hangzhou Institute of Technology, Xidian University. I received my bachelor's degree from the School of Mathematics and Statistics, Guangdong University of Technology.

My research interests focus on Embodied Agents and Continual Learning. I am enthusiastic about building agents that can perceive, act and keep learning in the physical world.

🌟 Newest update: I have just started my master's journey — looking forward to sharing my research on embodied agents and continual learning soon!

🔎 Research

“Let agents learn and grow, endlessly, in the physical world.”

  • Embodied Agent — perception, planning and decision-making of agents in embodied environments.
  • Continual Learning — enabling models to learn new tasks without forgetting old ones.
